# GoalVoice AI Privacy Policy Draft

GoalVoice AI collects your email address for login through Supabase Auth.

The app stores user goals, daily check-in transcripts, AI-generated daily analyses, and weekly progress reports.

Voice audio may be temporarily processed for transcription. Audio is not permanently stored in this MVP.

OpenAI is used for transcription and AI analysis. Supabase is used for authentication and database storage.

Users can delete their stored app data from the Settings page.

AI feedback may be imperfect and should be treated as guidance only.

GoalVoice AI is a productivity and accountability app. It does not provide medical, mental-health, therapy, legal, or financial advice.
